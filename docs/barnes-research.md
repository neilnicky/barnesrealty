## 2. Observations from public brand material

- **Wordmark.** "BARNES" in a high-contrast Didone with fine hairlines and ball-less bracketed serifs. The same letters are reused in the Instagram roundel monogram.
- **Website UI typography.** Raleway is used everywhere: headings in UPPERCASE with 2 px tracking at weight 400–500, body 16/24. Ink colour `#1D1D1B` on white.
- **Colour, by computed frequency on the homepage.**

  | Colour | Value |
  |---|---|
  | Ink | `#1D1D1B` |
  | Near-black | `#141414` |
  | White | — |
  | Greys | `#C0C0C0`, `#808080` |
  | **BARNES red** | `#B4002F` and `#C70A33`, used on the search button, CTAs and cookie consent |
  | Warm stone | `#EAE6DD` |
  | Blush paper | `#F7F3F2` |
- **Instagram highlight covers.** These are the strongest graphic motif found. Each cover is a giant Didone capital (S, H, A, R, E) in ink on warm cream, sliced by a **solid red bar**.
- **Instagram post language.** Posts are 4:5 crops and mostly drone reels or interior walk-throughs. Captions sit bottom-right in small Didone, for example "New York City, NY / 111 West 57th Street / For rent" with a thin arrow.
- **Photography.** Bright, daylight, wide-angle real estate photography: aerials of estates and pools, symmetrical interiors. Every listing photo carries a translucent BARNES roundel watermark, centred low. That watermark is the brand's own mark and is kept as-is.
- **Tone of voice.** Measured and courteous, and very French in its references ("art de vivre", "authentic deed", "piano nobile"). It avoids hype and leans on service and discretion.

## 3. Competitive notes (deliberately small)

| Brand | What was seen | Take-away for BARNES |
|---|---|---|
| Christie's International Real Estate (christiesrealestate.com) | Playfair Display serif headline over a full-bleed interior; a search bar overlaid in the hero; a gold "Contact Us" button | The category convention is a serif headline plus a search field in the hero. Fake search fields that can't return results are what the brief forbids. |
| Engel & Völkers (engelvoelkers.com/fr) | Proprietary serif; a hero video with a location tag and price; a red search button; heavy "sell for the best price" messaging; a "Private Office" offer | Red-accent CTAs are common at this tier. The differentiator is restraint and editorial pacing, not the colour alone. |
| Sotheby's International Realty (sothebysrealty.com) | **Not inspected.** The site served a bot challenge to headless Chromium. | — |

Opportunity: none of the observed competitors use scroll-choreographed editorial storytelling. BARNES's Didone and red-bar system is distinctive and under-used on its own site, which relies on a generic carousel and an uppercase Raleway layout.

## 4. Reasonable creative interpretations

These are labelled as interpretation, not fact.

- The Instagram red bar is promoted to the site's single accent, called "the red rule". It is used as a divider, an active-state mark and a reveal device.
- Two-tone Didone headings (ink plus muted) adapt FIND's two-tone device to the BARNES voice.
- Copy is lightly edited from verified sources. Examples: "One consultant, from valuation to the authentic deed" summarises the commitments page; "Find yours." comes from the Instagram bio.

## 6. Assets and rights

- **Listing photographs.** 1920–2999 px originals (stored as 2000 px masters) from `assets.barnes-international.com`, captured from each listing's own gallery folder (filtered by the listing UUID so "similar property" images are excluded). They are stored in `src/assets/properties/{ref}/` and tracked in `src/content/asset-manifest.ts`. **Rights:** © BARNES / listing agents. They are used for this client pitch only and need the client's confirmation before public launch.
- **Logo.** `public/brand/barnes-wordmark.svg` was traced with potrace from the official `logo-blanc.png` (400×80): 4× bicubic upscale, σ4 blur, then threshold. It closely matches the original, hairlines included. The client should replace it with master artwork.
- **Fonts.** Raleway is the brand's web font. Bodoni Moda (SIL OFL) is a display stand-in for the wordmark's Didone, used for headlines. It is a near match but not the proprietary logotype, and it is noted as such.
